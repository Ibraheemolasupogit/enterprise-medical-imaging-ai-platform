# Segmentation Baseline Model Card

This platform is a research and engineering demonstrator. Outputs are intended for technical evaluation and human review only and must not be used for clinical diagnosis or patient-management decisions.

## Model Purpose

The Milestone 8 model is a small MONAI 3D U-Net for synthetic lesion segmentation engineering
experiments. It exists to prove training, checkpointing, inference, evaluation, and governance
interfaces.

## Intended Use

Use only with repository-generated synthetic engineering fixtures for local research engineering
validation. Outputs require human engineering review.

## Excluded Use

Do not use this model for clinical lesion segmentation, adrenal-tumour diagnosis, malignancy
classification, radiologist triage, NHS deployment, medical-device workflows, or patient-management
decisions.

## Training Data

Training uses synthetic CT-like volumes and synthetic lesion masks generated by the repository. No
real patient data, public dataset, pretrained model, or downloaded model artefact is used.

## Evaluation Data

Validation and test samples are synthetic and split by research subject. Negative cases are retained.
Synthetic test metrics are not clinical-performance evidence.

## Architecture

The baseline uses MONAI `UNet` with one image channel and one binary lesion output channel. Default
configuration uses small channels and strides for CPU verification.

## Metrics

Evidence includes Dice, IoU, precision, recall, sensitivity, specificity, false-positive voxels,
false-negative voxels, volume error, Hausdorff 95, and average surface distance. Metrics are
reported separately for aggregate, positive-case, negative-case, and scenario-level views where
available.

## Limitations

The model is expected to overfit or underfit synthetic patterns. It does not represent scanner
diversity, contrast phases, acquisition protocols, pathology diversity, or annotation variability.
Exact floating-point results may vary across hardware and PyTorch builds.

## Safety And Ethics

Synthetic metrics must not be misrepresented as clinical evidence. Any future use with public
de-identified data requires dataset provenance review, subject-level splitting, model-change control,
and human oversight.

## Human Oversight

Human review is mandatory for all outputs. Empty predictions, false negatives, false positives, and
failed quality gates must block downstream research use until reviewed.

## Known Failure Modes

- Training/test leakage if future datasets ignore subject-level splitting.
- Image/mask misalignment.
- False-negative segmentation.
- False-positive segmentation.
- Threshold or post-processing choices that remove valid lesion voxels.
- Checkpoint/configuration mismatch.
- Localisation error propagation in future ROI-based workflows.

## Versioning

Initial model-card version: `m8-segmentation-v1`.
