# Synthetic CT Engineering Dataset Card

## Dataset

Synthetic CT-like engineering fixtures generated by this repository.

## Intended Use

The dataset is intended for testing repository mechanics, future pipeline contracts, deterministic manifests, checksum validation, and subject-level split validation.

## Excluded Use

The dataset must not be used for clinical diagnosis, patient management, medical-device validation, anatomical realism claims, or clinical-performance claims.

## Data Description

The generated arrays are small 3D NumPy volumes with simplified body, internal-structure, adrenal-region placeholder, and lesion-mask geometry.

They are not clinically realistic CT scans. Synthetic lesion shapes do not represent the full diversity of real pathology.

## Provenance

Generated locally from configuration in `config/data.yaml`. No public datasets or patient data are downloaded or used.

## Splitting

Splits are deterministic and research-subject-level. A research subject must not appear in more than one split.

## Limitations

- No DICOM or NIfTI data.
- No real scanner metadata.
- No clinically meaningful anatomy.
- No real disease labels.
- No support for clinical-performance claims.
